# 🇵🇪 Peruvian Ancestry & Juridical Lineage Engine

## 1. Primary National & Regional Archival Repositories

### A. Civil Vital Statistics & Identification Registries
1. **🏛️ RENIEC (Registro Nacional de Identificación y Estado Civil):**
   * **Online Search & Download Portal:** [RENIEC Actas Certificadas](https://apps.reniec.gob.pe/actascertificadas)
   * **Scope:** 20th and 21st-century civil registration records (Births, Marriages, Deaths) migrated from municipal registries nationwide.
   * **Capabilities:** Real-time online verification by subject name and date; immediate download of certified digital certificates (*Copia Certificada con Firma Digital*) via *Págalo.pe*.
2. **🏛️ Municipal Civil Registries (Arequipa & Lima):**
   * **Municipalidad Provincial de Arequipa (Registro Civil):** Custodian of physical manuscript registers for Arequipa urban center and Yanahuara (1860–1996). Non-migrated historic books accessible via municipal archival search.
   * **Lima Civil Archives:** Historic municipal books covering Lima center, Miraflores, Barranco, and San Isidro.
3. **🏛️ FamilySearch DGS Digital Microfilms:**
   * *Arequipa Civil Registration (1860–2017):* Collection ID `2224533` / `DGS 004703090–004703099` (Pino, Torres, Romani folios). Note: restricted rolls accessible via FamilySearch Center / Affiliate Library networks.
   * *Lima Civil Registration (1874–1996):* Microfilm series covering civil and judicial declarations.

### B. Catholic Parish Registries (1603–1992)
1. **⛪ Archivo Arzobispal de Arequipa & Parishes:**
   * Registers for Sagrario (Cathedral), Yanahuara (*San Juan Bautista*), Cayma, and Paucarpata.
2. **⛪ Archivo Arzobispal de Lima (AAL) & Parroquia El Sagrario / San Marcelo:**
   * `DGS 004278100–004278150` covering baptismal, marriage (*matrimonios y dispensas*), and burial registers connecting the Simons, Torres, and Pino family branches.

### C. Historical Notarial, Estate & Probate Archives
1. **📜 Archivo General de la Nación del Perú (AGN):**
   * **Digital Repository:** [AGN Repositorio Histórico Digital](https://repositorio.agn.gob.pe/) & [Servicios AGN](https://www.gob.pe/agn)
   * **Holdings:** Colonial and Republican *Protocolos Notariales* (wills, marriage contracts, property transfers, dowries), *Bienes de Difuntos* (probate), and *Causas Civiles y Criminales*.
2. **📜 Archivo Regional de Arequipa (ARA):**
   * **Portal:** [archivoregional.regionarequipa.gob.pe](https://archivoregional.regionarequipa.gob.pe/)
   * **Holdings:** Arequipa notarial indices (16th–20th century), agricultural estate titles (*fincas*), judicial litigations, and water rights contracts.
3. **📜 SUNARP (Superintendencia Nacional de los Registros Públicos):**
   * **Portals:** [SUNARP Plataforma](https://www.sunarp.gob.pe/) & [Servicio de Publicidad Registral en Línea (SPRL)](https://spr.sunarp.gob.pe/)
   * **Holdings:** *Registro de Personas Naturales* — public registries of Wills (*Testamentos*), Intestate Successions (*Sucesiones Intestadas*), and Real Property Ownership (*Propiedad Inmueble*).

### D. Historical Press, Hemerotecas & Gazette Archives
1. **📰 Biblioteca Nacional del Perú (BNP):**
   * **Digital Library & Hemeroteca:** [Biblioteca Digital BNP](https://bibliotecadigital.bnp.gob.pe) & [Memoria Perú](https://memoriaperu.bnp.gob.pe)
   * **Holdings:** Digitized newspapers (*El Comercio*, *La Prensa*, *El Peruano* gazette) for obituaries (*esquelas*), legal summons, and societal notices.
2. **📰 Universidad Católica San Pablo (UCSP) — Archivo Hemerográfico de Arequipa:**
   * **Holdings:** Centennial digitized Arequipa regional newspapers:
     * ***El Deber*** (1890–1962) — Key Catholic conservative daily covering Arequipa legal, ecclesiastical, and social family milestones.
     * ***La Bolsa*** (19th/20th century) & ***El Republicano***.

### E. Electoral & Juridical Lineage Registries
1. **🗳️ Jurado Nacional de Elecciones (JNE) — Infogob:**
   * **Portal:** [Infogob JNE](https://infogob.jne.gob.pe/)
   * **Scope:** Historical electoral rolls, citizen data verification, birthplaces, and public service records.
2. **⚖️ Appellate & Juridical Records:**
   * *Corte Suprema de Justicia del Perú* (Advocate Dr. José Andrés Pino).
   * *Colegio de Abogados de Lima (CAL)* & *Colegio de Abogados de Arequipa (CAA)* (Matriculation and legal practice records).
   * *Corte Interamericana de Derechos Humanos (CIDH)* (Special Agent Dr. Adrián Simons Pino).

### F. Transatlantic Colonial Lineage & US Diaspora
1. **🇪🇸 PARES (Portal de Archivos Españoles):**
   * *Archivo General de Indias (AGI)* & *Archivo Histórico Nacional (AHN)*: *Virreinato del Perú*, *Real Audiencia de Lima*, and *Catálogo de Pasajeros a Indias*.
2. **🇺🇸 US NARA Alien Case Files (A-Files) & Immigration:**
   * National Archives & Records Administration (NARA RG 85 / RG 59) case files for Peruvian family diaspora in Maryland (Oscar Torres, Glen Echo MD).
